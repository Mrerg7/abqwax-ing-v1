A first Brazilian wax is more intimidating in anticipation than in practice. Knowing what to expect removes most of the worry.

## Before the appointment

Let hair grow to about a quarter inch — roughly two to three weeks of growth, or about the length of a grain of rice. Hair that is too short will not grip, and hair that is too long is more uncomfortable to remove. Gently exfoliate a day or two before, and skip caffeine the day of if you are sensitive to discomfort.

## During the appointment

A Brazilian removes hair from the front, back, and in between. A skilled technician works in small sections and uses hard wax, which grips the hair rather than the skin. Most appointments take fifteen to thirty minutes. The discomfort is brief and sharp, and it lessens noticeably with each visit.

## Aftercare

Wear loose, breathable clothing home. For the first twenty-four hours, avoid hot tubs, saunas, intense workouts, and tight clothing. After a couple of days, begin gentle exfoliation to prevent ingrown hairs, and moisturize daily.

## Why consistency helps

Most people find the second visit dramatically easier. Hair grows back finer with a consistent schedule, and the body adjusts to the sensation.
