The short answer is three to four weeks. Because waxing removes the entire hair from the root — unlike shaving, which cuts it at the surface — regrowth is slower, softer, and less stubbly.

## Why three to four weeks

A hair's growth cycle has three phases, and not every hair is in the same phase at the same time. Waxing removes the hairs that are actively growing, and the rest surface on their own schedule. That is why a consistent routine matters: after several sessions, hair tends to grow back finer and more synchronized, making each appointment faster.

## What affects the timeline

- **Hair type and coarseness:** thicker hair can regrow more quickly.
- **Consistency:** regular appointments on a fixed schedule produce longer, smoother results.
- **Aftercare:** gentle exfoliation a few days after each session helps prevent ingrowns and keeps skin smooth.
- **Body area:** some areas, like the face, can regrow a bit sooner than others.

## How to stretch the results

Exfoliate lightly two or three times a week once skin has settled, moisturize daily, and avoid shaving between appointments — shaving resets the cycle and brings back stubble within days.
